# ContextInput 0.2.8 (Build 29)

- Set a default Hebrew or English input language for each app in Settings → App Defaults.
- Choose a running app or browse to an installed `.app`. Select **Use conversation** to remove its default.
- Existing Hebrew or English text in the focused field takes precedence over the app default. When the field is empty or unclear, the app default wins over nearby conversation text and terminal fallback. Other apps retain their previous behavior.
- Preferences are stored locally by bundle identifier and survive app updates.
